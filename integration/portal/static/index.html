Processing your report…

What happens next

  1. Your report is prepared

    Text and voice notes are transcribed, photos are checked and faces are blurred.

  2. The photo is analysed

    The problem type and how serious it looks are identified and verified.

  3. The right department is chosen

    Your report is routed and matched with the municipal procedure that applies.

  4. You get a tracking ID

    The report is saved and you can follow its status at any time.

Tips for a good report

  • Take the photo in daylight and close enough to show the damage clearly.
  • Keep people, licence plates and house numbers out of the frame when you can.
  • Mention the street or a nearby landmark so the team can find the spot.
  • If a report is unclear, it is not dropped: a municipal agent reviews it.
