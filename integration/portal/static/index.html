What happens after you send it

From your phone to the right municipal team, in four steps.

  1. We prepare it

    Text and voice notes are transcribed, photos are checked and faces are blurred.

  2. We analyse the photo

    The problem type and how serious it looks are identified and verified.

  3. We route it

    Your complaint goes to the right department with the municipal procedure that applies.

  4. You get a tracking ID

    The complaint is saved and you can follow its status at any time.

Tips for a good complaint

  • Take the photo in daylight and close enough to show the damage clearly.
  • Keep people, licence plates and house numbers out of the frame when you can.
  • Mention the street or a nearby landmark so the team can find the spot.
  • If a complaint is unclear, it is not dropped: a municipal agent reviews it.
