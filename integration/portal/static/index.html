What happens after you send it

From your phone to the end of the work, in four steps.

  1. We prepare it

    Text is cleaned, voice notes are transcribed, photos are checked and faces are blurred.

  2. We analyse it

    The problem type and how serious it looks are identified, then checked by fixed rules.

  3. Your municipality checks it

    It confirms the complaint and assigns it to its own services, to STEG or to SONEDE.

  4. You follow the work

    Each step appears here: checked, in progress, resolved.

Tips for a good complaint

  • Take the photo in daylight and close enough to show the damage clearly.
  • Keep people, licence plates and house numbers out of the frame when you can.
  • Mention the street or a nearby landmark so the team can find the spot.
  • If a complaint is unclear, it is not dropped: an agent of your municipality reviews it.
